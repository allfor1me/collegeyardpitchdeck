01 — The problem

University life is fragmented.

Students often have to search in different places for the things that shape their entire university experience.

  • 01 Off-campus accommodation
  • 02 Roommates
  • 03 Reliable information
  • 04 People they can trust
  • 05 Solutions to everyday university problems

Accommodation can be especially difficult.

  • Unverified listings
  • Unclear room availability
  • Outdated information
  • Difficult communication
  • Scattered information
  • Lack of trust

The problem is not that students lack options.

The problem is that the experience is fragmented.

04 — Trust

Trust comes first.

A hostel should not simply appear because someone submitted a listing. Hostels go through a verification process before becoming available to students. Only approved listings become visible in the student accommodation search.

  • OriginHostel ownerSubmits the property and its details.
  • GateVerificationCollege Yard reviews before anything goes live.
  • ResultApproved listingOnly approved hostels enter the search.
  • ReachStudentSees accommodation that has been checked.
  • OutcomeBookingRoom secured, receipt issued.

Verification is a process every listing must pass, not a claim about the state of the wider market. College Yard does not claim that every hostel in Abuja is currently verified.

05 — YardMate

Find the right person to share the space with.

Roommate matching built around compatibility.

YardMate helps students discover potential roommates based on information and preferences that matter when living together. It is designed to make roommate discovery more personal than simply matching two students who both need a room.

Design goal: personality and compatibility as a major part of the matching experience.

  • Level
  • Student information

Built around transactions and student services.

16 — Use of funds

Put people on the ground.

The ₦2.5M will help us mobilise people in Abuja to go directly to hostel accommodations and bring suitable hostels onto College Yard. The money is intended to support the real-world work required to build Campus Cribs' accommodation supply.

  1. 01 — FindPeople on the ground identify suitable hostel accommodations.
  2. 02 — ReachThey physically visit or contact hostel owners and managers.
  3. 03 — IntroduceThey introduce College Yard and explain the value of joining.
  4. 04 — OnboardThey help interested owners provide information and listing details.
  5. 05 — VerifySuitable accommodation moves through College Yard's verification process.
  6. 06 — ListApproved accommodation becomes available to students through Campus Cribs.

This is what turns College Yard from a digital product into a real-world accommodation network.

17 — Why this matters

A student platform needs something for students to find.

College Yard can have a great website. But if there are not enough quality accommodation options on the platform, students have less reason to use Campus Cribs. The ₦2.5M helps solve that supply problem.

  1. People on the ground
  2. More hostel conversations
  3. More potential hostels
  4. More verified accommodation
  5. More choice for students
  6. A stronger Campus Cribs launch

Not every hostel approached will join. These are potential hostels and targets — the goal is to build enough verified supply to make the launch worth a student's time.
